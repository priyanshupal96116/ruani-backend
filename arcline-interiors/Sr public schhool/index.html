About Our School

Education That Goes Beyond the Classroom

S.R. Public School is a senior secondary school located on Kalpi Road, Orai, Jalaun, Uttar Pradesh.

The school is focused on creating an environment that supports academic learning, discipline, confidence and the overall development of students.

Our aim is to help students build strong foundations while developing the skills and values they need for their future.
